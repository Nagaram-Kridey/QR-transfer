# ADR 001: Base45 for wire v2

Status: accepted for the initial profile.

Use RFC 9285 Base45 in QR alphanumeric mode. This provides a text-safe contract across QR readers.
Do not trim spaces. Raw-byte benchmarking is not on the critical implementation path; any later
change requires conformance evidence and a deliberate wire/profile revision.

Cost: some encoding overhead and larger QR symbols. Evidence: RFC known-answer examples and
Python/TypeScript byte-identical frame vectors, plus synthetic QR decoding. Physical trials pending.
